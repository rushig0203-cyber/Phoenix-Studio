and cartoon accounts are quality references, not a request to copy their work.

## Required end-state evidence

- Reachable stock workflow produces real, thematically coherent footage with
  intentional shot order, useful trims, appropriate framing, suitable preserved
  or selected sound, no unwanted posting-title overlays and provenance for every
  source. Requested social durations are retained; short assets are never looped
  to fabricate length. Preview/download, posting copy and hashtags work.
- Reachable cartoon workflow produces complete original characters and scenes,
  readable acting/action–reaction, meaningful camera compositions, controlled
  prop interaction and speech-related mouth/caption timing. Voice and music must
  be listened to; static pose tests do not prove finished-video quality.
- Manager directs a concrete opening, progression and payoff suited to the chosen
  medium, not generic unrelated filler. Posting copy reflects actual content.
- Rendering remains sequential/bounded and resumable on this laptop, with no
  large new local model. Queue progress/errors and final review remain useful.
- Representative full stock and cartoon MP4s are rendered, inspected and played
  from the product. Running build/worker identity matches the tested update.
  Automated tests alone do not satisfy this requirement.
- Source/docs/tests updated locally and Git, while private media/settings remain
  untracked. Instagram publishing/account changes require specific authorization.
