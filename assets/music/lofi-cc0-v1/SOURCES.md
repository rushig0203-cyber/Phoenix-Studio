# Recorded instrumental assets

Artist: Loyalty Freak Music. Album: **MELODIES WITH A BEAT**.

- [Composer's album and free download](https://loyaltyfreakmusic.com/music/melodies-with-a-beat/)
- [Original album listing and instrumental genre](https://chezmonplaisir.bandcamp.com/album/melodies-with-a-beat)
- [CC0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The composer's page links CC0, and its ZIP includes the retained `Licence CC0.txt`.
The dedication permits commercial use, modification and redistribution without
mandatory attribution. Preserve the composer's request against registering these
shared recordings as exclusive Content ID assets. Phoenix does not do that.

These six static production assets are 60-second excerpts starting 30 seconds into
their originals, converted to stereo AAC at 128 kbps. Together they occupy about
5.6 MiB. The source/archive and asset SHA-256 digests are in `manifest.json`.
No owner video, credential, model or runtime export belongs in this folder.

Mood labels are editorial choices. Low-frequency attacks in the pinned files
were measured offline with `scripts/measure-stock-music-onsets.cjs`; the compact
timestamps are stored in the manifest and checked against the actual recordings.
New recipes can start on a measured attack and fit nearby picture cuts to those
attacks. This does not establish exact tempo, musical downbeats, emotion, current
trends or Instagram catalogue availability. Automatic reels save their chosen
recording and native-tempo excerpt; legacy recipes retain their prior music.
Final listening review remains necessary.
